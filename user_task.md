# User Tutorials
Tutorials to teach users how to navigate and use all the features of My Favorite Albums.

---

## Navigating My Favorite Albums
To enter My Favorite Albums click the following link: [My Favorite Albums](https://cholstro.shinyapps.io/shiny-music/). Upon entering you will be greeted at the **Home** page.

![Tabs_labeled.png](/images/Tabs.png)

The **Home** tab displays the albums and artists in your database.

From this home screen 7 tabs are displayed: **Home**, **Number One Albums**, **Top Albums by Year**, **Artists’ Albums**,
**Favorite Artists**, **Artist Comparison**, and **Vinyl**.

To navigate to a different tab, simply click on the label. Greyed out lettering indicates the tab currently open.

---

## Features of My Favorite Albums

#### Number One Albums
The main controls of this feature are the two circular sliders located on either end of the timeline.

![slider_1993-98.png](/images/slider_1993-98.png)

The left slider is the bound for the lower year and the right slider is the bound for the higher year. Move either slider left and right to adjust the range of years to see.
For example to view number one albums from 2000-2015:
1)	Move the right circle slider right until the blue label above it reads 2015
2)	Move the left circle slider right until the blue label above it reads 2000

![slider_2000-15.png](/images/slider_2000-15.png)

As the sliders are moved, the listed albums beneath update. For 2000-2015, it should look something like this.

![slider_table.png](/images/slider_table.png)

#### Top Albums by Year
1) Click on the dropdown menu and select a year to display.

![top_drop.png](/images/top_drop.png)

2) Hit the **Submit** button to display the albums for that year.

![top_submit.png](/images/top_submit.png)

Below the submit button an album list appears.

![top_table.png](/images/top_table.png)

> Note: The submit button needs to be hit only once; subsequent changes in year automatically updates the list below.

#### Favorite Artists

![favorite_drop.png](/images/favorite_drop.png)

2) Choose if the database should count EPs and Live Albums in the count. With the checkmark green it excludes them, 
with it unchecked it includes them.

<span style="font-size: 1.1em;">**Included:**</span>

![favorite_submit.png](/images/favorite_submit.png)

![favorite_table.png](/images/favorite_table.png)

#### Artist Comparison
There are two dropdown menus to select from labeled **First band or artist** and **Second band or artist**.

![compare_drop.png](/images/compare_drop.png)

Click on the dropdowns to select the artists to compare against one another. The graph below automatically updates to display 
these two artists.

![compare_graph.png](/images/compare_graph.png)

Listed above the graph are the colors that each artist corresponds to. On the x-axis is the year and on the y-axis is the rating. 
This allows you to visualize your ratings between artists throughout the years of their albums.
